Who We Are

Designing Dreams Into Reality

With a passion for perfection and an eye for detail, Maverick Designs is Mohali's premier architecture & interior design studio.

We are Maverick Designs

Founded with a vision to redefine living spaces, Maverick Designs brings together world-class architecture and stunning interior design under one roof. Based in Kharar, Mohali, Punjab, we serve clients across the region with innovative designs that blend functionality with aesthetics.

Our team of talented architects, interior designers, and 3D visualization experts work collaboratively to create spaces that tell your story. From luxurious bedrooms and state-of-the-art kitchens to immersive gaming studios and cinematic home theatres — we do it all with unparalleled craftsmanship.

Every project is a masterpiece. Every detail matters. That's the Maverick promise.

How We Work

Our Design Process

A seamless journey from your initial idea to a beautifully realized space. Here's how we bring your vision to life.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your lifestyle, preferences, budget, and vision. This is where dreams take their first shape.

02

Concept & Design

Our designers create mood boards, floor plans, and initial concepts. We explore materials, colors, textures, and spatial layouts tailored to you.

03

3D Visualization

Experience your space before it's built! Our photorealistic 3D renders and virtual walkthroughs let you see every detail in stunning clarity.

04

Execution & Delivery

Our skilled craftsmen and project managers ensure flawless execution. We handle everything from procurement to installation with precision and care.
